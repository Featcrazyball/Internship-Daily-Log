A link to a website was given. This task consists of 2 sub-tasks.
    1. Look around the website and test user functionality.
    2. VAPT, standing for Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (optional)

While my role in the company is designated as Full Stack Engineer Intern, I was able to use skills learnt from 
the previous semester due to my specialisation in Cyber Security to do VAPT as well. 
The first 1 and a half hour, was downloading all the tools that I was familiar with onto my laptop (as 
they were not previously installed due to them being on tryhackme). Tools included Kali Linux, VMware Workstation, 
and a Windows 11 image file. While downloading, I set up my report, looked up information regarding the domain name, 
and poked the website around through simple SQL Injection testing, and looking through the Javascript of the website.

While reading, I found out that all of the website's API, 
were exposed. Including the sensitive ones that 
required authentication or even a higher privilege. 
Other findings included having no account lockout, no 2FA, and the IPv6 and IPv4 of the domain name.

Finally, when my tools finished downloading, I used Gobuster to find hidden directories and subdomains. 
While I was unable to find any sub domains, I found that the hidden directories were 
being redirected to an azure domain. Successfully allowing me to bypass cloudflare. 
Moreover, I found out that the frontend of the website is run on port 443 and 8443 
while the backend is ran on 80 and 8080 through nmap. Note that 8443 and 8080 are 
http alts and http proxy. Unfortunately, I was unable to find the OS and its version.
