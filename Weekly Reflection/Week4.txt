Compared to the previous 3 weeks, this week was significantly more 
eventful. This is the week that my accumulated efforts came to 
fruition with my Industry Supervisor's presentation of the web 
application to a Singapore telco company. To sum up the previous 
3 weeks, week 1 was about understanding the web application from 
the user's point of view. Week 2 was about seeing how Momentum Z 
functions as an organisation. Finally, Week 3 focused on the hosted 
web application's system architecture and, at the end of the week, 
getting into the codebase.

This week, I finally started fixing major vulnerabilities in the web 
application. At the beginning of the week, specifically Monday and Tuesday, 
the number of bugs I fixed almost reached 2 digits. Those bugs included 
Broken Function Level Authorisation (a bug where regular users could access 
admin pages and functions without proper permissions), a EULA Gate error 
(a bug where users couldn't log in properly), and a few bugs where some 
components were not loading properly. I also implemented a bunch of features 
such as replacing placeholders in documents with their proper values stored 
in the web application, docx processing for Artificial Intelligence, and report 
generation.

Once again, as seen from the tasks I completed above, this week has been 
significantly more eventful compared to the previous 3 weeks. I would like 
to add that the most eventful day this week was Thursday. As the day right 
before the presentation on Friday, I had to look through the website again 
and list all the bugs. And somehow, fate decided to pull a prank, and I found a 
lot of issues regarding the features of the website. In the end, I had to work 
till late at night. I was fortunate that Tuesdays and Thursdays were work-from-home 
days, or else I would have wasted more time commuting instead of doing productive 
work.

This experience felt very similar to the night before a school project presentation, 
when everyone rushes to fix last-minute problems. It showed me that while school and 
work are very different environments, the same habits, like leaving testing until the 
end, lead to the same stressful outcomes. 

The key lesson I took away is the importance of testing and reviewing my work regularly 
rather than only right before a deadline. It also made me realise that even as an intern, 
my work directly affects real outcomes, since it was being presented to an actual client. 
Moving forward, I want to take more ownership of the quality of my work and prepare early 
so that I am ready when moments like this come.
